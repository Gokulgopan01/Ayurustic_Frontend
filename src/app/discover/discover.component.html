The Art of Botanical Silk

At Ayurustic, craftsmanship begins with material integrity.
We combine 100% mulberry silk with plant-based colour traditions
inspired by Ayurvastra practices. The result is silk shaped by
heritage and refined for modern living.

Botanical Silk Art

100% Mulberry Silk

We use only long-fibre mulberry silk, valued for its smooth
texture, strength, and natural sheen.
The fabric is lightweight, breathable, and fluid in drape —
offering comfort without compromising refinement

100% Mulberry Silk

Botanical Colour

Our silk is coloured using plant-based formulations prepared through a slow,
controlled process. Unlike conventional chemical dyes, botanical applications
allow the fabric to absorb tone gradually, producing depth and subtle variation.

No two pieces are identical. Slight tonal shifts are not flaws — they
are the natural signature of botanical dyeing.
